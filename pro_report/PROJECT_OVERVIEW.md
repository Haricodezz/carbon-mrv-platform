# Project Overview: Carbon MRV Platform

## Executive Summary
The Carbon Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (MRV) platform is a full-stack, enterprise-grade application aimed at digitizing and decentralizing the carbon credit lifecycle. It bridges the gap between agricultural/ecological projects and corporate ESG compliance by leveraging Machine Learning (ML), Satellite Verification, and Blockchain Technology.

## Project Purpose
To provide a transparent, scalable, and verifiable system for farmers, NGOs, and project developers to generate carbon credits, and for corporations to purchase and retire them for ESG compliance.

## Problem Solved
Traditional carbon credit markets are plagued by opacity, high verification costs, double counting, and fraud. This platform solves these issues through:
1. **Automated Verification:** Using ML and satellite data (NDVI, AGB) to assess biomass.
2. **Immutable Ledger:** Utilizing Web3 blockchain technology to prevent double-spending and ensure transparent credit lifecycles.
3. **Streamlined Economics:** Integrated fiat-to-crypto workflows via Razorpay.

## Platform Goals
- Democratize access to carbon markets for smallholder farmers and NGOs.
- Ensure 100% transparency using blockchain ledgers.
- Reduce auditing costs via AI-assisted satellite verification.
- Provide a seamless B2B marketplace for carbon credit acquisition.

## Current Maturity Level: Advanced MVP
The platform operates as an **Advanced Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**. It possesses all core functional loops (Auth, Project Creation, Auditing, Purchasing, Minting, Retiring). However, it relies on mock fallbacks for specific third-party systems (e.g., Razorpay/Web3 absent credentials) and requires rigorous stress-testing and legal compliance framing before commercial launch.

## Commercial Potential
Extremely high. The voluntary carbon market is expanding rapidly. This platform is perfectly positioned as a B2B SaaS for carbon project developers and sustainability-focused enterprises. With minimal modifications, it could serve as a white-label registry or a direct marketplace.
